Accuracy / Intercomparison Benchmarks
======================================

Planned tests
-------------
  vs_sources4c     Compare ALPHANSO yields and spectra against SOURCES-4C
                   benchmark cases for beam, homogeneous, and sandwich geometries.

  vs_vlaskin       Compare against experimental data from Vlaskin et al. for
                   16 alpha-emitting elements.

  vs_versions      Cross-check numerical agreement between ALPHANSO versions
                   to catch regressions (an_yield, energy spectra, mean energy).

This directory is a placeholder. Scripts will be added here as the
intercomparison work progresses.
